# Galactic Tower Defense

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). No install, no internet needed.

- **Menu:** pick a sector (map) and a difficulty, then Deploy.
- **Build:** keys `1`–`0` pick a unit, click the battlefield to place it (hold Shift to place several). Right click or `Esc` cancels.
- **Manage:** click a unit to upgrade (`U`), sell (`S`) or change targeting (`T`).
- **Waves:** `Space` launches the next wave. Launching early pays a bonus. `A` toggles auto waves, `F` changes speed, `P` pauses.
- **Legendary Protocols:** `Q` Orbital Lance (click a target), `W` Stasis Pulse, `E` Overclock.
- Progress autosaves after each cleared wave. Achievements and best records are kept in your browser.

Files: `index.html` (layout), `css/style.css` (look), `js/core.js` (helpers, sound, saving), `js/data.js` (units, enemies, waves, sectors), `js/entities.js` (game objects), `js/game.js` (game loop and rules), `js/ui.js` (menus and controls).
