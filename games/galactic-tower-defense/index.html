Field Manual

The Void Swarm is advancing along the hyperlanes toward your core. Deploy units beside the lane to stop them. Every enemy that reaches the core drains its integrity. Lose it all and the sector falls.

Controls

  • 1–0 pick a unit, then click the battlefield to deploy it. Right click or Esc cancels.
  • Click a deployed unit to upgrade it (U), sell it (S) or change its targeting (T).
  • Space launches the next wave. Launching early, while enemies are still on the field, pays a bonus.
  • Q W E trigger Legendary Protocols. F changes speed, P pauses, A toggles auto waves, M mutes.

Economy

  • Each cleared wave pays 5% interest on banked credits (up to 60).
  • Selling refunds 70% of what you invested, or 100% if the unit was placed during the current build phase.
  • Your progress autosaves after every cleared wave.
