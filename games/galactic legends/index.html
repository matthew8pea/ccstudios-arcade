Halloween Event

Before you go in

The fighters you take into this event are the REAL characters in your roster. Any of them can die here, and a fighter who dies is removed from your roster.

  • Six rounds against the Burnt Legion. General Hawlo holds the third, Lord Trick the last.
  • Health carries from round to round. Everyone still standing is healed when a boss falls.
  • If your fighter dies you take over a teammate. The run ends when the whole team is down, and nothing is saved if you leave.

Fallen fighters can be bought back in the Shop. Skeleton Warriors are already dead: one that falls is out for the run and back for the next.
